# Route trade-off audit

The current synthetic endpoints yield an almost direct valid path: 642.4 m
against a 639.2 m direct distance (detour ratio 1.005). The direct segment does
not encounter a hard building or statutory-protection exclusion after applying
the default 45 m centreline building envelope.

Temporary deterministic profiles were tested with environmental/water weights
of 0/0.4 (shortest), 2/7 (balanced), and 12/30 (environmental). All produced
the same 642.4 m geometry, 508.3 m2 environmental right-of-way overlap, and no
water crossing. Pairwise Hausdorff distance and length differences were 0 m.

This means the current open-land connection has no competitive low-cost passage:
the mapped environmental area near the alignment is not avoidable at a defensible
length cost inside the connected hard-exclusion space. Alternatives are therefore
reported as converged rather than drawn with invented offsets. This is an
early-stage scenario limitation, not an environmental finding or engineering
conclusion.
